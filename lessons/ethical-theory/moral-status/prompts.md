# Image-generation prompts — The Ambassador's Manifest (Moral Status)

Hand-authored 16-bit SVGs are in `assets/`. Use these prompts to generate richer
raster (PNG) versions if desired; keep the aesthetic consistent with the other
lessons. Swap the `.svg` references in `index.html` to `.png` after generating.

Shared style suffix for every prompt:
> 16-bit SNES-era pixel art, limited palette, dark indigo background (#1d2235),
> crisp pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no text, centered subject, 4:5 portrait crop.

## ship
The survey ship *Charitable Interpretation*: a boxy long-hulled research vessel
drifting in a starfield, one hull window lit warm gold (the status officer's
office) while the rest are dark blue, a dish antenna on top, green engine glow at
the stern, a ringed violet planet behind it.

## manifest
A cluttered officer's desk: six fanned manila dossier folders stacked at left, each
with a red UNFILED tab, a heavy inked rubber stamp standing at right, and three
empty wire sorting trays labeled only by color — green, amber, red.

## thrum
The Thrum in a containment cell: a soft, boneless, eyeless pink creature curled on
the floor, a small puckered mouth its only feature, fine cilia along its base,
trembling, with red pain-waves radiating outward and a spiking vitals monitor in
the corner.

## spore
A Kessel spore: a smooth green seed pod, seam-lined, resting on a low pedestal in a
dim lab; behind it a large translucent blue ghost-outline of the tall rational
adult it will become; an hourglass on the floor and a flatlined monitor nearby.

## fading — reused for **the Ninth Sun**
A Kessel hospice alcove that is also a shrine: an ancient alien lying still on a
bier under a violet blanket, eyes closed, ridged crest; a votive lamp burning
bright gold above the bed and never allowed to go out; an open book of devotions
on a stand beside it; a heart-rate monitor in the corner beating steadily.
(The existing panel already reads this way — the lit lamp is a votive, not the
mind-lamp of the earlier draft.)

## loom
The Loom: a tall standing machine intelligence built as a brass weaving frame, gold
warp threads strung down its body, a single calm blue lens where a face would be,
one jointed arm extended to write on a hanging purple tapestry covered in lines of
argument. A welfare gauge beside it reads flat zero. It should read unmistakably as
a *machine* — that is the point of the dossier, since its appearance is doing work
its makers' certificate cannot.

## glim
A Glim: a small crystalline flower of pale green light drifting just above a pair of
cupped gloved crew hands in a dim cargo bay, its glow spilling down onto a bed of
moss that is visibly growing toward it, tiny sparks drifting off its petals. It is
the last of its kind, and the last living thing from a burnt world.

## tessera (new — hand-authored SVG in assets/, no PNG yet)
A crystalline alien on an airless moonlet: a lattice of pale blue-white tiles
growing on bare grey rock under a black star-field, one region grown into a clean
resolved pattern of even tiles and one region still jagged, dim, and unresolved; a
single detached shard drifting away from the mass; a blank instrument panel below
reading nothing at all.

## vorl
The Vorl: a dense swarm of hundreds of identical small amber beetle-units, arranged
so that the darker units among them form the unmistakable pattern of a calm,
thoughtful face; a few stragglers break away at the edges; below, one lone unit
sits isolated and inert.

## declassified
A dossier page on a desk with its black redaction bars peeling up and drifting away
like leaves, revealing beneath them a small pixel Earth; a translucent red
DECLASSIFIED stamp struck diagonally across the page.

## circle
The moral circle: concentric rectangular rings in pixel style — an innermost pink
core, a green ring, a blue ring, and an outermost ring still drawn as a broken
dashed line; small creature-marks inhabit each ring, and a row of six differently
colored creatures waits outside the outermost ring.

## bentham / kant / singer (relic cards — copied from other lessons)
See the utilitarianism and kantian-deontology lessons' `prompts.md`. Bentham → a
felicific-calculus tablet; Kant → a starry sky over a compass; Singer → an
expanding circle.
