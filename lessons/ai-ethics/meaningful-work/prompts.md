# Image prompts — AI and the Right to Work ("The Last Job")

Twenty panels ship as square PNGs in `assets/`, made as **five 2×2 sheets**
and split into quadrants. Rawls's idea scene is copied from
`lessons/bioethics/right-to-health-care/assets/idea-rawls.png`. Each sheet
below is one short, complete prompt, ready to paste into any image generator.
Render each sheet square. Inspect every panel full-size: reject any with
baked-in text, and re-roll any sheet that repeats a panel instead of drawing
four different scenes.

**To re-roll one panel on its own,** paste the style line, then that panel's
sentence, then "One single square image, not a grid."

The setting is an original, cheerful retro-future: the sky-town of Hammock
Heights in 2150, where robots do every job. Sheets A and B are the town.
Sheet C is our world today. Sheets D and E are idea scenes for the thinker
cards. Rules:

1. **Original designs only.** No likeness to any cartoon, film, show, or game
   (no Jetsons, Futurama, WALL-E, or Rosie the Robot look-alikes). Don't name
   any of them in a prompt.
2. **Gentle humor, no menace.** Robots are helpful, polite, and a little
   apologetic. Nobody is a villain, and nobody is mocked.
3. **Paper and signs stay blank.** The petition, picket signs, screens, and
   labels carry no writing.
4. **No portraits of real people.** Every philosopher gets an idea scene
   without a face. The picket line and the port show unnamed people.
5. **Vary the people.** Every panel with people names each figure's age,
   gender, and skin color, and every crowd is mixed: men and women, young and
   old, a range of skin tones. Paste each recurring character's description
   word for word.
