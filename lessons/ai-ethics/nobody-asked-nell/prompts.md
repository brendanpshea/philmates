# Image prompts — Group Privacy ("Nobody Asked Nell")

Twenty scenes ship as 512×512 PNGs in `/assets/`, generated as **five 2×2
sheets** and split into quadrants. Each sheet below is one complete prompt,
ready to paste: house style, grid wrapper, and the four panels in order. See
[docs/image-generation.md](../../../docs/image-generation.md). Inspect every
panel full-size: reject any with baked-in text.

The cast is **invented**: one careful woman and four talking gadgets. Six
rules:

1. **Gadgets are characters, not products.** Each has two round dot eyes and a
   simple mouth. No brand shapes, no logos, no real product designs. Don't name
   a company or a product in a prompt.
2. **Screens and labels are blank.** A screen shows a plain glow, simple
   shapes, or rows of small colored squares. Never letters, numbers, or icons
   that look like an app.
3. **The gadgets are nosy, not evil.** Cheerful, chatty, a bit too interested.
   No red glowing eyes, no menace. The lesson asks whether anyone did wrong, so
   the art must not answer it.
4. **Other people are simple figures.** Crowds and neighbors are small,
   round-headed cartoon figures with dot eyes, in varied skin tones. Photos of
   people show a plain smiley face. Nell is the only portrait.
5. **The Havasupai panel has no people and no cultural imagery.** It shows
   sample vials and nothing else. No feathers, no desert clichés, no canyon.
   The slide is serious and the image must not decorate it.
6. **No portraits of real people.** Alan Westin and the group-privacy theorists
   get idea-emblem relic cards.

## Palette note

This lesson is the **toy-shop gadget** look: glossy rounded plastic devices in
the four PhilMates accent colors, set against Nell's warm lamplit cottage.
Gadget glow is cool info-blue; Nell's world is warm amber. That keeps it apart
from the lair violet of The Villain's Ledger and the phosphor green of The
Network With No Middle.

## Relic cards (hand-authored SVG, 80×108)

Standard frame: dark border, accent inner frame, rank gem in the top bar,
rarity pips at the bottom. These are not generated.

### `relic-westin.svg` — Alan Westin (accent: info-blue `#4cc2ff`)
Emblem: a hand resting on a tap, with a single drop falling from the spout.
Encodes privacy as control: you decide when, how, and how much about you
reaches others (*Privacy and Freedom*, 1967).

### `relic-group.svg` — group privacy (accent: go-green `#46e07a`)
Emblem: many small dots arranged so that together they form one keyhole.
Encodes the claim from Floridi, Taylor, and van der Sloot's *Group Privacy*
(2017) that some privacy interests belong to the pattern, not to any one dot.

## Alt-text reminder

Write `alt` from what the final PNG shows, not from the prompt. Counts are the
usual failure: check for exactly five linked figures, two green figures in the
crowd, six lookalikes with one blank, four cameras and five houses, and six
figures under the canopy before shipping.
