# Image prompts — Right to Health Care ("The Ship's Chest")

Sixteen scenes ship as square PNGs in `/assets/`, generated as **four 2×2
sheets** and split into quadrants. Each sheet below is one short, complete
prompt in a code block, ready to paste into any image generator. Render each
sheet square. Inspect every panel full-size: reject any with baked-in text, and
re-roll any sheet that repeats a panel instead of drawing four different
scenes.

**To re-roll one panel on its own,** paste the style line, then that panel's
sentence, then "One single square image, not a grid."

The prompts are kept short on purpose. Long prompts make the model lose track
of which panel is which and repeat scenes. Each panel is one sentence that
starts with its subject.

The frame borrows a ship's doctor, a captain, a one-legged cook, and a
marooned sailor from Robert Louis Stevenson's *Treasure Island* (1883, public
domain). The story is set in Bristol before the ship sails, while the crew
signs its articles. Seven rules:

1. **Original designs only.** No likeness to any actor or to any film, TV,
   animated, or game adaptation. Don't name the characters, the novel, the
   author, or any adaptation in a prompt.
2. **No pirate menace.** No Jolly Roger, no skull-and-crossbones, no drawn
   cutlasses or pistols, no eye patches, no blood. This is a working crew
   arguing about money. Nothing in the art should decide who is right.
3. **Disability with dignity.** The cook is strong, busy, and good at his job.
   His missing leg and crutch are drawn plainly, never as menace or a joke.
   The same goes for the boy recovering from illness and the dying sailor.
4. **Medicine is a bottle and a cup.** No saws, no amputation, no surgery, no
   needles, no wounds.
5. **Paper stays blank.** The articles, the ledger, and every label are blank.
6. **No portraits of real people.** Norman Daniels, John Rawls, H. Tristram
   Engelhardt, and Albert Jonsen get idea-emblem relic cards. Mill reuses his
   card from the utilitarianism lesson.
7. **The child in the Oregon case is never drawn.** That slide has no art.
8. **Vary the people.** Every panel with people names each figure's age,
   gender, and skin color, and every crowd is mixed: men and women, young and
   old, a range of skin tones. The doctor is Black in every panel he appears
   in, so paste his description word for word, even in a crowd.

## Status (2026-10-21)

All sixteen panels are in `assets/` and their alt text matches what was drawn.
Eight came from sheets A to D. The other eight were re-rolled under rule 8 with
**Sheets E and F**, because the first round drew every sailor in those panels
as a white man and the doctor as white. The source sheets are kept in
`temp/right-to-health-care-sheets/`, outside `assets/`, so they don't double
the size of the SCORM zip. Save any new sheets there too, and update the `alt`
text after any re-roll.

Known drift: in `the-vote` the cook has a beard and a red bandana, while his
portrait is clean-shaven. The four relic cards are hand-authored SVGs.


|---|---|---|---|

| Bottom-left | `squall.png` | Health vs. health care | Nobody can stop the storm, so nobody can owe you health. People can owe you shelter and care. |
| Bottom-right | `the-rigging.png` | The opportunity range | Every station on the ship is a path a sailor might take. The cook's lost leg closed the rigging to him. |

## Relic cards (hand-authored SVG, 80×108)

Use the standard frame: dark border, accent inner frame, rank gem in the top bar,
rarity pips at the bottom. See `lessons/ethical-theory/*/assets/` for reference.
These are not generated.

### `relic-daniels.svg` — Norman Daniels (accent: go-green `#46e07a`)
Emblem: a fan of five paths spreading out from a small harbor, one of them
broken and bridged by a splint. Encodes his argument: illness shrinks your
share of the range of lives open to you, and health care mends the path.

### `relic-rawls.svg` — John Rawls (accent: ink `#eef1ff`)
Emblem: three starting blocks on one level beam, held up by posts of
different heights over uneven ground, with a star ahead. Encodes fair equality
of opportunity: where you start should not decide where you can get to.

### `relic-engelhardt.svg` — H. Tristram Engelhardt Jr. (accent: magenta `#ff6ad5`)
Emblem: a lottery drum with a storm cloud inside it. Encodes the natural
lottery: bad luck from nature is unfortunate, but it is not unfair, because
nobody did it to you.

### `relic-jonsen.svg` — Albert Jonsen (accent: info-blue `#4cc2ff`)
Emblem: a life ring on dark water. Encodes the rule of rescue: the pull to save
the one person in front of us, whatever it costs.

### `relic-mill.svg` — copied from `lessons/ethical-theory/utilitarianism/assets/mill.svg` (accent: amber)
