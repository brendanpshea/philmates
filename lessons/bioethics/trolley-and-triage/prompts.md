# Image prompts — The Switch and the Scalpel ("Trolley Problems & Medical Triage")

The lesson ships with **generated 16-bit pixel art** (`/assets/*.png`) sliced from two 2x2 grids.

## House style (prepend to every prompt)

> **16-bit SNES/JRPG pixel art**, Victorian-industrial railway meets sterile emergency clinic. Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, subtle dithered shading, chunky silhouettes that read clearly at 64 px. Tight palette drawn from the PhilMates UI: void background `#11131f`, panel slate `#1d2235`, ink `#eef1ff`, and four accent inks — go-green `#46e07a`, magenta `#ff6ad5`, info-blue `#4cc2ff`, amber `#ffcf5a`. **No text, no lettering, no watermark, no signature.** Transparent or `#11131f` background. Square (1:1).
